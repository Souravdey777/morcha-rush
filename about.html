About Morcha Rush

Run the march. Outrun the corruption.

What is Morcha Rush?

Morcha Rush is a free, three-lane 3D endless runner in the spirit of Subway Surfers and Temple Run, set at a street march (a morcha) on a festive Indian road strung with marigolds. You ring a gate's doorbell, shout "BHAAG!" and run, dodging barricades, Section 144 signs, potholes and VIP convoys while a bribe-hungry Daroga Ji chases you for his chai-paani.

It plays instantly in any modern browser on desktop, Android or iPhone. There is nothing to download and no sign-up. On a phone, add it to your home screen and it opens full-screen like an app.

Satire, not politics

The humour pokes at everyday red tape and petty corruption: forms in triplicate, roads whose budget went missing, power cuts at the worst moment. It never targets real people or parties. Every character and name is made up, and nothing here is meant to hurt anyone's sentiments.

What's in the game

  • Five playable characters, each with a catchphrase and a special ability.
  • Indian street power-ups: Cutting Chai, Dhol Beat, Press Pass, Jugaad Thela and Steel Thali.
  • Random Bijli Gayi power cuts, onion carts that make you cry, and a trap burger.
  • Achievements, coins to unlock runners, and a best-distance score to beat.

How it was made

Morcha Rush is built with Three.js and WebGL, with 3D characters and street props, and runs as a plain web page with no build step. The source code is open on GitHub. It was developed by Sourav Dey.

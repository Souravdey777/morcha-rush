Morcha Rush Characters

Five runners, each with a catchphrase and a special move. Start as Guddu for free and unlock the rest with the coins you grab on the road.

Guddu (RE-EXAM!)

Third attempt. Second leak. Still has the admit card laminated.

Ability: none. Just hope.
Unlock: free

Thali Dadi (THAN-THAN!)

Brought her steel thali from home. Didn't bring it back.

Chappal Swat: automatically swats a barricade out of the way every 8 seconds.
Unlock: 250 coins

MC Dhol (DHAM!)

Dropped a diss track at the barricade. Dhol is the beat, Jantar Mantar is the studio.

Beat Drop: every landing sends a shockwave that clears obstacles ahead.
Unlock: 500 coins

Camera Person (LIKE & SHARE!)

Rolling since the first barricade. The reel outlived the minister.

Viral Flash: every near-miss flashes the camera for +25 points.
Unlock: 1000 coins

Cocky (UNBOTHERED!)

Chronically online, 11 hours a day, bathroom breaks included. Lazy only in the physical sense.

Unkillable: shrugs off one crash per run.
Unlock: 1500 coins

👮 Daroga Ji (CHALO THANE!)

Still waiting for your Form 26-A. Attested. Three copies. Daroga Ji chases every runner down the street. Crash twice in a row and he catches you, though 500 coins of chai-paani will buy you one more chance per run. Learn how to keep him back in the how to play guide.

Which character is best?

Cocky is the most forgiving, because one free crash per run saves long runs. MC Dhol suits players who jump a lot, since every landing clears the lane ahead. Camera Person rewards risky, close dodges with extra points, and Thali Dadi is the cheapest upgrade from Guddu, clearing barricades on a timer.

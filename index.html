HOW TO PLAY

The run

🏃
Run the marchRing the gate bell and BHAAG! Switch lanes, jump and slide. Collect ₹ coins to unlock new runners.
🚨
Daroga JiCrash once and you stumble: he closes in for a few seconds. Crash again before you recover and he catches you. Caught? Pay him ₹500 from your wallet to carry on, once per run.
⌨️
ControlsDesktop: ← → or A D to switch lanes, ↑ / W to jump, ↓ / S to slide, P to pause. Phone: swipe left, right, up or down.

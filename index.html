HOW TO PLAY

The run

🏃
Run the marchRaise the tiranga at Jantar Mantar and BHAAG! Switch lanes, jump and slide. Collect coins to unlock new runners.
🚨
Daroga JiCrash once and you stumble: he closes in for a few seconds. Crash again before you recover and he catches you. Caught? Pay him 500 coins to carry on, once per run.
⌨️
ControlsDesktop: ← → or A D to switch lanes, ↑ / W to jump, ↓ / S to slide, P to pause. Phone: swipe left, right, up or down.

Obstacles

🚧
Barricade"Adjusting, please wait." Jump over it.
🚫
Section 144 signSlide under it.
🕳️
PotholeThe road budget went somewhere. Jump over it.
🧅
Onion cartJump over it. Hit one and you will be in tears.
🚓
VIP convoyRoute diverted. Too big to jump: switch lanes.
🚉
Station closedInconvenience regretted. Too tall to jump, no gap to slide: switch lanes.

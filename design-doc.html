Morcha Rush 🥁

Run the march. Outrun the corruption. Delhi-style festival chaos, one swipe at a time.

Pitch

Subway Surfers meets a festival street march in Delhi. It's a race against corruption: the jokes are about bribes ("chai-paani"), vanished road budgets, missing files, VIP culture and red tape, never real people or groups. Googly eyes, bouncy physics, nobody gets hurt.

Core loop

  • Auto-run on 3 lanes, speed ramps up
  • Swipe or arrow keys: lane, jump, slide
  • Collect ₹ coins, unlock characters
  • Hit once: you stumble and the Inspector closes in. Hit again before recovering: game over

Controls

  • ← → / A D : change lane
  • ↑ / W : jump · ↓ / S : slide
  • Touch: swipe in 4 directions
  • Space: start / restart

Platform & tech

  • 3D: one HTML file using Three.js (loaded from a CDN), low-poly models built in code, no image files
  • Chase camera behind the runner; the Inspector is visible over your shoulder when he closes in
  • requestAnimationFrame with delta time, targeting 60fps; shadows and pixel ratio scale down on phones
  • Scales to fit the window, touch first · high score + unlocks saved in localStorage

Obstacles

  • 🚧 Barricade, "ADJUSTING, PLEASE WAIT": jump it
  • 🪧 Section 144 board, tall with a gap underneath: slide
  • 🕳️ Pothole with "Work in Progress ✨" flag: jump
  • 🚨 VIP Convoy: lane flashes "ROUTE DIVERTED" as a warning, then blocks it. Switch lanes

Power-ups (with HUD timers)

  • ☕ Cutting Chai (5s): speed burst + steam trail
  • 🥁 Dhol Beat (6s): smash through barricades
  • 📸 Press Pass (4s): invincible, screen flashes
  • 🛺 Jugaad Thela: wobbly hover-cart that absorbs one crash

Juice

  • Screen shake on a crash
  • "+10" floats up on coins
  • Comic SFX text: DISHOOM! OYE! CHALO! TADAA!
  • Bursts of confetti and marigold particles
  • Parallax: India Gate, domes, autos, DTC buses, chai stalls, pigeons

Screens

  • Start: title, tagline, Play, character picker (locked ones show their ₹ price)
  • HUD: distance (m), ₹ coins, power-up timer rings, Inspector proximity meter
  • Game over: distance, coins, best score, Restart

Tone guardrails

  • All characters fictional and generic. No real politicians, parties, causes or uniforms
  • No violence. A crash is a cartoon stumble with stars over the head
  • The punchlines are signs, forms, potholes and diversions

Out of scope for v1

  • Audio files (SFX are on-screen text, optionally tiny WebAudio blips)
  • Missions / daily challenges
  • Online leaderboard

How to Play Morcha Rush

Ring the gate bell, shout "BHAAG!" and run. Switch between three lanes, jump and slide past red tape, and keep Daroga Ji off your heels for as long as you can.

Daroga Ji, the chaser

Crash once and you stumble: Daroga Ji closes in for a few seconds. Crash again before you recover and he catches you. Caught? Pay him 500 coins of chai-paani to carry on, once per run.

Watch out for

  • 📘 Required Reading: a rare book worth +50 coins.
  • 🍔 Shiny burger: looks like a power-up, but it is a trap. Bad optics slow you down for 2 seconds.
  • ⚡ Bijli Gayi: random power cuts plunge the street into darkness for 5 to 15 seconds.
  • 🪳 The little brown visitor: shows up once a run. Tap it for a bonus and an achievement.

Tips to score higher

  • Pick a runner whose ability fits your style. Cocky shrugs off one crash per run; MC Dhol's landings clear obstacles ahead. See all characters.
  • Near-misses with Camera Person are worth +25 points each, so play close.
  • Save the Steel Thali for when you are stumbling: it resets the chase.
  • During a power cut, stay in the middle lane so you have room to dodge either way.
  • Skip the shiny burger. Every time.

FAQ

Is Morcha Rush free?

Yes. It is completely free and runs in your browser with no download or sign-up.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes. Swipe to play, and add it to your home screen to open it full-screen like an app.

How do I unlock new characters?

Collect coins. Thali Dadi costs 250, MC Dhol 500, Camera Person 1000 and Cocky 1500. Guddu is free.

Is it like Subway Surfers?

It is a three-lane endless runner in the same spirit, set on a festive Indian street with satirical obstacles.
